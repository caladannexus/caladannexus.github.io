Experience

Operating experience, not generic consulting.

Caladan Consulting is led by Higini Hernández, whose career in seafood has centred on bluefin tuna since 2010.

Experience spans Southern and Atlantic bluefin, ranching, superfrozen processing, sourcing, international sales and distribution across Asia, Europe and North America. It includes the development of superfrozen Southern bluefin operations in Singapore, international sales leadership at Balfegó, processing and facility-development work in Spain, and business development in the U.S. premium and superfrozen seafood market.

Current advisory work also extends to Pacific bluefin opportunities and capture-based aquaculture.

Approach

Selective, independent and commercially grounded.

Caladan undertakes a limited number of assignments where specialist bluefin knowledge and commercial judgement materially affect an investment or operating decision.

Engagements can range from an early opportunity assessment to detailed due diligence, project development and implementation support.
